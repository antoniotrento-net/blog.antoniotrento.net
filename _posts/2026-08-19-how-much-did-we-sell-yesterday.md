## The most expensive question in the company

It's Monday morning. The owner walks in and asks something simple: "how much did we sell yesterday?". Ten minutes pass. Someone opens the ERP, someone a spreadsheet, someone the e-commerce. Three different numbers come out. None is wrong — they're just three different definitions of "sold".

This is the real software you're missing. Not another report. A **screen** that answers in twenty seconds, with a shared definition of what counts as a sale.

## Where the numbers went

ERP, e-commerce, spreadsheets, ads, bank, and the sales reps' heads. Six sources, six truths. As long as they stay separate, every answer is a small investigation.
