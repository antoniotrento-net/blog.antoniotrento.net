Your company's numbers live in six different places: ERP, e-commerce, spreadsheets, bank, ads, people's heads. When you need an answer — how much did we sell yesterday? — nobody has it in twenty seconds. This guide collects the articles that explain, with no fluff, how to turn scattered data into a product that gets opened and used.
