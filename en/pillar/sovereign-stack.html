"It's on my server, so it's fine" isn't enough. The difference between a workflow that runs and one that leaks data is the architecture: where the prompts go, what the vendor sees, who holds the keys. This guide collects the articles on building an AI stack that stays yours — Docker, n8n, RAG, agents — without depending on an opaque cloud.
